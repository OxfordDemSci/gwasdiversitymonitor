Figure 1 | Growth and representation in the GWAS Diversity Monitor, 2005–2026. (A) Annual numbers of unique GWAS publications (bars) and GWAS Catalog accessions (line), with the repository release and 2020 publication of the original Nature Genetics Correspondence marked; hatching denotes the partial 2026 year. (B) Comparison of the original Correspondence with the current snapshot: published GWAS increased from 4,346 to 7,797, whereas the European share of recorded participant instances changed from 88.45% to 88.20%. (C) Annual discovery and replication participant instances on a logarithmic scale. Participant instances are summed across accessions and do not represent deduplicated individuals. (D) Annual ancestry composition of discovery and replication participant instances combined; marker area scales with the within-year participant share. (E) Annual non-European participant share for discovery, replication and all stages from 2010 onward; the dashed line is the cumulative non-European estimate reported in 2020. (F) Complementary cumulative distributions compare the percentage of all ancestry-linked funders and cohorts involved in at least a given number of unique publications. (G,H) Current non-European share for the ten funders and cohorts linked to the most GWAS publications, respectively. (I) Lorenz curves directly compare the concentration of unique linked-publication credits across all retained funders and ancestry-linked cohorts; legend annotations report the Gini coefficient and top-ten share. (J,K) Non-European share versus linked publication count for every ancestry-linked cohort and retained funder, respectively. Funder and cohort attribution use full counting: a publication is credited to every linked funder or cohort. Cohort panels therefore summarize the ancestry of linked GWAS and should not be interpreted as estimates of cohort demographic composition. Ancestry categories are the harmonized broad categories used by the GWAS Diversity Monitor from NHGRI–EBI GWAS Catalog population metadata. Historical annual values are reconstructed from publication dates in the current snapshot and can change with retrospective Catalog curation. Data snapshot: 30 August 2026; latest included publication date: 2026-07-30.
